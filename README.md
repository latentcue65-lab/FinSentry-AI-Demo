# FinSentry AI

## Client Demo and Project Brief

**Governed AI support for banking investigations and operations.**

FinSentry AI brings case evidence, advisory AI findings, policy constraints and
human approvals into one investigation workspace. Existing banking systems
remain the systems of record. AI investigates and recommends; authorized people
make decisions within policy.

### What the Demo Shows

- A payment investigation connected to evidence, policy and case context.
- Missing screening input and an advisory path that abstains.
- Separate maker and checker roles, including a self-approval restriction.
- An illustrated approval to maintain an existing hold, not release funds.
- An audit trail with the case still under investigation.

### Important Boundaries

This is a recorded, guided demonstration using synthetic sandbox data, with
labeled explanatory diagrams. It is not a live client environment, a production
banking deployment or proof that every documented integration was executed.

The video does not demonstrate sanctions clearance, a validated risk score, a
final credit decision, a regulatory filing or autonomous payment release. It
does not establish regulatory certification, measured business savings or a
fully passing repository-wide verification gate.

This public repository contains only client-facing documentation and selected
finished media. Application source code, credentials, raw captures, internal
logs, customer records and deployment configuration are not included.

5 October 2026; it is not a claim of a newly executed banking workflow.
